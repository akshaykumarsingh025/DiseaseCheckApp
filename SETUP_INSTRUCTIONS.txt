  1. RAZORPAY - Appointment Booking Payments
===============================================================================

WHAT IT DOES: Patients pay for OPD consultations (₹111), AI Diet Plans (₹299),
and Remove Ads (₹149) through Razorpay inside the app.

STEPS:
  a. Go to https://dashboard.razorpay.com/sign-up
  b. Sign up with your business email and phone.
  c. Complete KYC (PAN, bank account, business details).
     - Business type: Individual / Sole Proprietor (or your clinic type)
     - Category: Healthcare / Medical
  d. After KYC is approved, go to Settings > API Keys.
  e. Generate a new key pair.
  f. Copy the "Key ID" (starts with "rzp_test_" or "rzp_live_").

===============================================================================
  5. ANDROID APP SIGNING (for Play Store)
===============================================================================

To upload to Play Store, you need a signing keystore.

STEPS:
  a. Generate a keystore (or let Play Console handle it with "App signing
     by Google Play" - recommended for first upload).
  b. If generating yourself:
     keytool -genkey -v -keystore diseasecheck.jks -keyalg RSA \
       -keysize 2048 -validity 10000 -alias diseasecheck
  c. Create a file: android/key.properties with:
     storePassword=YOUR_KEYSTORE_PASSWORD
     keyPassword=YOUR_KEY_PASSWORD
     keyAlias=diseasecheck
     storeFile=diseasecheck.jks
  d. Place the .jks file in: android/app/diseasecheck.jks

  [ ] .jks keystore file placed? (Yes/No)
